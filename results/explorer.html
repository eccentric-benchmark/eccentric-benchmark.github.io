How to read the results. The logical error rate is the fraction of shots in which any logical qubit failed over all syndrome-extraction rounds (by default as many rounds as the code distance), with a 95% Wilson confidence interval. The per-round rate assumes independent rounds: ε = (1 − (1 − 2P)1/rounds)/2. Shots are sampled until 100 logical errors, 100,000 shots or one hour of decoding.

Status. measured at least 100 logical errors; low statistics stopped by the shot or time limit before 100 errors; upper bound no logical error seen, the 95% upper bound is given; does not fit the code needs more qubits than the device has; decoder not applicable e.g. matching on a code that is not matchable. "Qubits" counts the code's data and ancilla qubits, "circuit qubits" all qubits of the compiled circuit.
